Case study 01

Merchandising Agent

I built a retail intelligence agent that turns transaction and inventory data into clear merchandising decisions. The system reads sales performance, identifies stock risk, highlights excess inventory, and recommends pricing actions using a transparent rule-based engine instead of opaque black-box logic.

The goal was to help merchandising teams move faster: instead of manually digging through reports, they get a readable operational view of what is performing, what is under pressure, and what should be adjusted.
